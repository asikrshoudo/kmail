# Kmail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Kmail is an email client made by TheKami. It connects to the email accounts you already have. TheKami does not provide email accounts or mailbox hosting.

## What Kmail does with your data

- **Connections.** Kmail connects directly from your device to the IMAP and SMTP servers you enter. TheKami has no server in between and cannot see your mail, your passwords or your contacts.
- **Passwords.** Your mailbox passwords are encrypted with a key held by the Android Keystore and stored only on your device. They are sent only to your own mail provider, to sign in.
- **Mail on your device.** Message headers you have synced are stored in a local database on your device. Message bodies you open are kept in memory only.
- **App Lock.** If you turn it on, a salted hash of your PIN is stored on your device. The PIN itself is never stored.
- **Attachments.** Files you attach are read only when you pick them and are sent to the recipients you choose. Files you save are written where you choose.
- **Remote content.** Images and other content from the internet inside emails are blocked by default. If you allow them for a message, your device loads them from the sender's servers.

## What Kmail does not do

- It does not collect analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
- It does not show ads.
- It does not share data with third parties. It includes no third-party tracking code.
- It does not upload your contacts.

## Deleting your data

Removing an account in Settings deletes its saved password and the mail cached on your device. Uninstalling Kmail deletes all data it stored. Your mailbox on the provider's server is never changed by removing an account.

## Children

Kmail is not directed at children under 13.
